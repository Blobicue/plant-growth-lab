T1 CONTRACT AUDIT AND DERIVATION

Source-backed identity audit
juvenile-allocation30/legacy/model.gd:_insert appends a node, stores parent index,
position, kind and explicit birth=tick. _connections requires parent before child;
the collar at index0 also has explicit lifecycle/reserve meaning. Thus arbitrary
permutation without reference remapping/topology preservation is NOT an engine
symmetry. transport_pools never reads birth or uses a physiological age priority.
At all11 archived births the7 nodes share a birth tick. Swapping equal-age siblings
and their indices while preserving collar0, parents, physical coordinates, tissues,
resources and tip references is representation-only. Earlier growth, links, spatial
competition or real birth times are not rewritten by this test.

The generic T1 kernel is tested under arbitrary isomorphic graph permutations,
including cycles. The engine still supports its original paid parent trees; the
integration test uses a valid topological permutation only. Plant-loop reversal
is an additional separate check. The source/audit does not claim all other engine
subsystems are independent of arbitrary node numbering.

From ordered transfer to simultaneous diffusion
Original: each of3 sequential passes/hour evaluates F=.24*a*(Xi-Xj) on values
already modified by earlier edges. a=min(fractional solid mass of endpoints).
No concentration volume, donor priority, resource-demand signal or transport-energy
cost appears in the source. Its literal finite-step behavior is part of the old
contract, retained unchanged in the baseline and every old branch.

T1 defines a NEW time-discretization interpretation of that local law. Let g=.72*a
per hour, equal to .24*a per1/3hour in the nominal first-order approximation. Let L
be the weighted graph Laplacian on existing connected paid edges. For frozen graph
and masses during the transport stage, dX/dt=-L X; no maintenance or growth is
interleaved within this stage. Integrate exactly1 model hour using N simultaneous
explicit steps M=I-L/N. Primary requested N=24; numerical shadow N=48. On unusually
high-degree test graphs N_eff=max(N,1+floor(max row sum of g)). The real plant tree
has much lower degree, but the bound is enforced rather than assumed.

For each node, retain (1-sum_j g_ij/N_eff)*Xi and send g_ij*Xi/N_eff to each neighbor,
all computed from the SAME frozen substep amounts. This paired counterflow has net
edge flux g_ij*(Xi-Xj)/N_eff, so it implements the original amount-gradient intent.
Both outgoing and incoming terms are nonnegative. No incoming receipt is re-spent
until the next substep. Strict row sum/N_eff<1 bounds every sender's total outgoing
stock and makes each output a convex combination of the previous component values.
Isolated/disconnected tissue retains its amount. No resource clamping or mass
repair is used. Symmetric rates give unit column sums as well as row sums: each C,
N,W pool is conserved, including its corresponding chemical energy in mobile C.

For an isomorphism/permutation P, L'=P L P^T, hence
 M' P X = P M X and (M')^N P X = P M^N X.
This is the equivariance guarantee in exact arithmetic. Code sorts edges by their
physical endpoint keys only to make floating-point summation reproducible. Sorting
does not select earlier recipients because all sends read frozen amounts. Fixture
checks compare exact canonical values after independent node and edge permutations;
engine comparisons remap node references to physical positions and apply unchanged
scientific tolerances, not raw ID-dependent hashes.

Rate versus finite-step response
Keeping .72/hour does not keep the old3-step finite response. For a full-strength
two-node edge, D=X1-X2 obeys:
 old3 ordered passes: D(1h)/D(0)=(1-2*.24)^3=.140608;
 T1 at N steps: (1-1.44/N)^N; continuous limit exp(-1.44).
Thus T1 is not an increased nominal rate or extra hour, but it is a different
finite-time operator. Matching the old multiplier with an exact continuous edge
law would require a DIFFERENT conductance, -1.5*ln(.52), which was not adopted.
No claim is made that .72 is a uniquely measured biological conductance. The
choice is justified as a transparent, conservative first-order interpretation of
the existing local exchange intent, not as an exact restoration of unknown biology.
On networks neither operator is uniformly 'faster' at every particular recipient.

Numerical evidence
fixtures.py independently builds the symmetric L, computes its eigensolution at1h,
and compares N=3,6,12,24,48,96 with total time and g fixed. Automatic CFL substeps
are recorded, including the high-degree64 star.70 profiles cover stars, chains,
cycles, disconnected/isolated vertices, zero edges, uneven tissue fractions,
empty stores, heterogeneous stocks, uniform equilibrium and1e-18 stocks. The fixed
N24 normalized L1 error limit is2% of total pool; convergence at24/48/96 is checked.
Godot is compared with independent Python transfer arithmetic and checks budgets,
conservation, positivity, maximum principle and420 exact canonical permutations.
These tests precede biological results and do not use seed success to choose N.

The same11 biological origins receive both primary24 and fixed refinement48 for
768h. A change in discrete root/survival conclusions under refinement will be
reported as numerical uncertainty, not resolved by selecting the better run.
Material C,N,W all follow T1, so any biological effect cannot be assigned uniquely
to N delivery. Applying T1 to all plants at a legacy birth checkpoint also allows
changes in competitors and parents; results are conditional model comparisons,
not a target-only root manipulation or ordinary T1 population history.
