Existing contract
Three in-place sequential passes/hour: F_ij=.24*a_ij*(X_i-X_j), where
 a_ij=min(solidC_i/PACK_i,solidC_j/PACK_j), X in mobile C,N,W.
Only connected active paid parent-child edges exist. An edge can reuse resources
received earlier in the same pass. Equal amounts, not defined concentrations.
This is ordered edge splitting, not a simultaneous diffusion step. The comment
'Pairwise finite-volume exchange, equal/opposite, no edge to an unbuilt cell' and
amount-gradient law justify examining a simultaneous local diffusion alternative;
they do not establish a calibrated species or unique continuous-time physiology.

Selected alternative (fixed before outcomes)
Interpret the nominal edge coefficient over a1/3h pass as conductance
 g_ij=.72*a_ij per model hour. Hold paid topology/solid masses fixed during the
hour's transport stage. Integrate dX_i/dt=sum_j g_ij*(X_j-X_i) for ONE model hour
with simultaneous explicit Euler substeps, requested N=24 (eight subdivisions of
each old pass). This resolution is chosen for numerical accuracy, not seed success.
Use N_eff=max(N,1+floor(max_i sum_j g_ij)) so all sender retention weights are
strictly nonnegative; disconnected/isolated nodes have zero row sum. dt=1/N_eff h.
For every substep, freeze all X. Each directed edge sends (g_ij*dt)*X_i from i to j;
sender retained stock is (1-dt*sum_j g_ij)*X_i. Sum all received transfers only
AFTER frozen sends. Net edge flux equals g_ij*dt*(X_i-X_j). Outgoing amount never
exceeds pre-substep stock; receipts cannot be re-spent within that substep.

This convex averaging conserves each pool, preserves nonnegativity and bounds by
the previous connected-component min/max. C movement creates no C, heat or chemical
energy. No new edges, volumes, pumps, routing goals, demands or biological costs.
Canonical physical-position ordering is used ONLY for reproducible floating-point
summation; all fluxes use frozen state, so order cannot provide first access.
Generic kernel tests permit arbitrary vertex/edge permutations and cycles; actual
plants keep their existing paid tree and connectivity rules. No cycles are added.

Important discretization limitation

full-strength two-node edge, the old hourly difference multiplier is(1-.48)^3;
T1 gives(1-1.44/N_eff)^N_eff, approaching exp(-1.44). Thus T1 does NOT preserve the
old finite-step mixing speed; it preserves its nominal first-order flux coefficient.
It generally mixes this two-node case less aggressively. Do not calibrate g upward
to match the old multiplier, add transport time, or describe T1 as an exact bug fix.
