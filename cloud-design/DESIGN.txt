LOCAL NITROGEN CONTRACT — DESIGN ASSESSMENT, NOT IMPLEMENTATION

Decision
Recommend one isolated test of maintenance-first construction feedback: a local
root nitrogen shortfall can temporarily inhibit optional stem/leaf construction.
Do not add a directed nitrogen pump, larger seed, uptake bonus, longer bud lifetime,
or recycling simultaneously. This is a new allocation policy requiring a new
experimental contract, not a correction to W27 or a promotion of candidate30.
No simulation or production change was made for this assessment.

Existing executable contract and units
Locations: juvenile-allocation30/legacy/model.gd (transport_pools, maintain,
growth payment), world.gd (step and shared uptake/allocation), reserve.gd.
Amounts below are model C, N and W mass units; they are not measured grams.
One tick is one model hour. A tissue packet contains solid C m; NR is N/C.

  kind       PACK[C]   NR[N/C]  UPKEEP[1/hour]  yield[C solid/C paid]
  stem       .0024     .024     .0011          1
  root       .0018     .060     .00375         1
  leaf       .0012     .110     .0116          .8

Construction debits at the proposal's existing paid front are exactly
  (C,N,W) = (PACK/yield, PACK*NR, .3*PACK).
Root bill = (.0018,.000108,.00054); stem N bill = .0000576.
No withdrawal from a whole-plant pool pays a front's bill. Leaf conversion loss
is respired to atmosphere with heat, not erased.

Only active connected paid parent–child edges transport material. Three sequential
in-place passes per hour use, separately for X in {C,N,W},
  k_ij=.24*min(m_i/PACK_i,m_j/PACK_j), F_ij=k_ij*(X_i-X_j),
  X_i <- X_i-F_ij; X_j <- X_j+F_ij.
F is mass transferred per pass; k is dimensionless per pass. This equalizes
AMOUNTS, not concentrations in defined volumes. The implementation is not a
calibrated physical diffusion law. There is no demand signal, new unbuilt edge,
or explicit active-pump energy charge. Sequential node order can matter; prior
whole-plant reversal checks do not prove invariance to equivalent graph relabeling.

Connected roots request finite shared-soil receipts:
  W_request=floor(min(.7*m,max(0,.012-W))*1e9),
  N_request=floor(min(.1*m,max(0,.003-N))*1e9).
Requests/receipts are integer quanta (1e-9 mass). Existing proportional allocation,
sorted claims and rotating remainder ties govern competing plants. These caps are
per hourly request, not extra resources. No proposed policy may bypass receipts.

Per-node hourly maintenance demand is
  d=m*UPKEEP*(1+1.8*(1-W/(W+.002))).
Turnover consumes up to .35*d solid C into litter, releasing associated bound N/W
locally. The remaining .65*d is paid from mobile C; unpaid demand adds stress.
Fully paid tissue can spend additional C recovering stress. Active connected tissue
then repairs by r=min(missing,C*yield,N/NR,W/.3), debiting r/yield C, r*NR N,
.3*r W. Living tips also respire .00001 C/hour. Death occurs above stress45 or
below20% packet mass. Repair and upkeep already precede optional construction.
Maintenance is not an unlimited guarantee: each node must actually pay.

Hour order: soil receipts, light, transport, shared-air allocation/photosynthesis,

Candidate30 defers new reproductive collection below32 paid nodes; reserve

Derived budget: why a decision experiment is plausible
For seed12011/id3 at its first N-only refusal, total connected mobile N=.001086223
across9 packets. A frozen equal-amount calculation gives .000120691 per packet,
above the root bill. Paying one hypothetical stem and creating a tenth compartment
gives (.001086223-.0000576)/10=.000102862, below that bill.
The accompanying frozen-pool-accounting.json covers all11 cohorts. This arithmetic
ignores ongoing maintenance, uptake, graph distances and deadlines: it is NOT a
counterfactual trajectory or proof that the root can receive that N. It identifies
an avoidable expenditure/dilution hypothesis worth separating from transport.
N allocated into growth is unavailable for later upkeep/repair; even a policy that
preserves payment priority can worsen future maintenance indirectly.

Competing hypotheses and discriminating evidence
A. Construction feedback (recommended): finite N is spent on cheaper shoot packets
before a root front accumulates its bill. Predict less early nonroot N expenditure,
then greater front N and paid root construction, before any survival difference.
Costs: less new leaf area, delayed stems, lost buds and continued tip respiration.
An outcome-only survival improvement does not establish this mechanism.

B. Transport/geometry: slow local mixing or the equal-amount compartment model,
rather than investment choice, causes the front deficit. Connected tissue does
not imply rapid access; no broken early connection was observed. A future volume-
based or simultaneous flux operator would change the transport contract and need
its own conservation, nonnegativity and ordering assessment. Increasing k or passes
now would conflate capacity with decision-making.

C. Active root-directed routing: add antisymmetric edge flow u_ij to existing F_N,
limited by donor availability after maintenance/repair. It must never teleport N.
A proposed energy budget would require E_act=epsilon_N*sum_edges(abs(u_ij)),
C_respired=E_act/20, with corresponding atmospheric C and heat. epsilon_N has units
model energy/model N. The generic N pool has no chemical species or electrochemical
potential from which to identify epsilon_N. A zero-cost pump or a fitted coefficient
would therefore add an unjustified advantage; donor floors also change allocation.
Reject this as the first experiment, not as a claim that plants lack active transport.

D. Uptake sensing: change acquisition according to whole-plant demand. This acts at
the soil boundary, unlike internal allocation. Late suppression of competitors'
uptake cannot release their legacy N stores (about72–75% of world N at first-gen2
births). Earlier regulation could change history, but requires a separate assay;
the completed late control does not falsify all acquisition feedback.

E. Germination/endowment: larger reserves, a different initial root/shoot structure,
or delayed germination could change establishment. All descendants received the
same paid start; this is not a payment bug. Any larger endowment must be charged to
the parent, with fewer/delayed offspring as a possible consequence. Changing seed
strategy would confound the internal-allocation hypothesis.

F. Environment: spatial soil N/W, competition, shade and limited recycling can
restrict establishment even with ideal allocation. One gen2 was shaded at birth;
shade also lowers water demand, so removing shade is not an established net benefit.
Changing site selection, resource fields or recycling belongs in distinct models.
Do not remove competitors or replenish fields to demonstrate this policy's success.

Biological interpretation: primary research only
[1] Ruffel et al. (2011), PNAS, doi:10.1073/pnas.1108684108.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22025711/
Split-root experiments distinguish local and systemic N-supply/demand signaling
in root foraging. This supports investigating status-dependent decisions, not
our Boolean rule, transport speed, cost constants or a guaranteed benefit.
[2] Ota et al. (2020), Nature Communications, doi:10.1038/s41467-020-14440-8.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14440-8
Arabidopsis experiments identify shoot-status signaling through CEPDL2 affecting
root nitrate acquisition/transport. Acquisition signaling is not evidence for a
free internal bulk-N pump or a calibrated coefficient in this generic model.
[3] Krouk et al. (2010), Developmental Cell, doi:10.1016/j.devcel.2010.05.008.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20627075/
NRT1.1 links nitrate and auxin transport; the authors propose repression of lateral
root growth under low nitrate. This cautions against a universal “low N means
more roots” rule. The recommended controller is a falsifiable abstraction of
allocation feedback, not a reproduction of these molecular mechanisms.

ONE next experiment — preregistered design, NOT RUN
Treatment: after existing maintenance and reserve collection, freeze all proposal
payment predicates. A connected live root proposal q emits D_q=1 exactly when
N_front<PACK_root*NR_root AND C_front>=PACK_root AND W_front>=.3*PACK_root.
It emits D_q=0 otherwise. Signals originate at existing paid fronts. Propagate
source-tagged, timestamped states over existing active connected edges, synchronously
at one edge per model hour. This new timing is an explicit model assumption, not
a measured biological rate; do not sweep it. Use newest source timestamps, expire
messages at the unchanged proposal deadline, propagate clears identically, and
invalidate messages through severed edges. Newly generated state can affect its
source front immediately; other fronts first see it on a later hourly step.
A front receiving any unexpired D=1 defers only stem/leaf construction. Roots still
must pay the original local bill. Normal creation, occupancy checks and14h expiry
continue; no retained buds, refunds, N reservation or extra transport. State and
buffers must be serialized and fingerprinted. No ancestry/seed/age-specific rule.

This introduces signaling as information-only within the existing coarse sensing
abstraction; it has no separately resolved molecular signaling cost. That is a
limitation, not a claim that biological signaling is free. It transports no C/N/W
and cannot supply a construction bill. A later detailed signaling-energy model
would be a separate contract, not an extra adjustable cost in this experiment.

Falsification/decision: signal receipt without increased front N and root payment
refutes this proposed pathway under the fixed protocol. More front N without paid
roots identifies another binding constraint. Survival without added roots does
not confirm the root-investment hypothesis. Root gains with lower C fixation,
more unpaid maintenance or harmed competitors must be reported as tradeoffs; they
cannot be erased by averaging. Even favorable root outcomes are mechanistic
support only: no claim of sustained replacement, no automatic promotion, and no
rescue tuning. A distinct preregistered all-plant holdout study would be required
before any broader viability claim.
