Accounting and the five negative cases
All carbon tables below use milli-model-C (1000*C), not grams. The window is0–768h.
F=fixation; U=paid upkeep respiration; tip respiration T=15.360 in EVERY arm;
Q=mobile C spent on tissue repair; V=structural turnover to litter; G=construction.
Operating O=F-U-T-Q; net mobile change O-G. Turnover V is not subtracted again:
Q already pays to replace it. Total respired C=U+T+(Q-V), because no new leaf packets
were constructed (so new-construction yield loss is zero). Q-V is repair yield
respiration, already inside Q. All33 histories close these identities, have zero
unpaid upkeep, and restore the full turnover mass. This separates successful
maintenance funded from seed stores from self-supporting operation.

Mechanism: water access plus a distinct shaded case
For four negative cases other than4242/5, light receipts are65.86368 model-E and
same-light fully-hydrated demand H=.269955347C. Their mean leaf water is.002172–
.002399, and W uptake.057844–.068431; positive three-root comparators12011/3 and

therefore do not separate positive from negative cases. The observed equation
reduces leaf demand by w/(w+.002), also caps it by available leaf water, and increases
upkeep by1+1.8*(1-w/(w+.002)). Water shortage depresses income while raising cost.

The five negative cohorts have identical aggregate photon receipts and H in their
paired baseline/T1 runs. T1 raises fixation by.008683–.013561C and raises mean leaf
water and W uptake in every one. Algebraically, the gain comes from a smaller gap
between H and actual water-limited demand; the atmosphere-claim/fixation gap is
only about2e-7C over768h. This supports water accessibility as the immediate budget
mechanism. It does not isolate how much came from changed graph transport, the
extra root, or changed surrounding plants/soil; all-plant T1 changes allC/N/W.

Does root-only N still bind?
Yes locally, but the OLD third-root stall is gone: all11 T1 third roots are paid
at age5. The age4 N-only delay lasts one hour. By age16 every T1 cohort's next
root front again has C>=.0018 and W>=.00054 but N<.000108. That fourth-root segment
expires at17h. Archived expiry states have localN=.000073734–.000094045 against
.000108 required, while whole connected mobileN=.000861264–.000983138. Whole-plant
C/N/W could cover one packet arithmetically; those pools are not instantly available
at the front under the transport law. This is direct local-access evidence, not
proof that redirecting N is free or that a feedback policy would help.
Eight cohorts also have an observed leaf N-only refusal at23h and expiry at30h:
localN at expiry.000066535–.000074376 versus leaf bill.000132, despite sufficient
whole pools. All eleven retain four leaves. Other fronts have mixed C/N or C-only
blocks. Positive cases also show N-only blocks, so this is not a sufficient
explanation of which budgets turn negative. At later times C becomes more limiting.
Event labels store only first occurrences, and daily refusal counters count calls,
not independent episodes. The expiry 'blocked' string is from the prior hour;
analysis separately checks the current local values and the age16 live-front log.
These distinctions prevent stale labels or overlapping counters from inflating
claims. No feedback or donor intervention is justified by stock arithmetic alone.

Quantitative break-even bounds (fixed observed tissue/environment costs)
For net-positive operation, F must exceed C*=U+T+Q. For nondepleting mobile stores
while paying the observed construction, F must exceed C*+G. The first threshold
is necessary for operating surplus; neither ensures local bills or reproduction.
