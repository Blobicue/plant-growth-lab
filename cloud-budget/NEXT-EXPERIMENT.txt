F1 — PREREGISTERED FRESH-FOUNDER TRANSPORT COMPARISON (NOT EXECUTED)

Question
Does the juvenile budget/accessibility pattern persist when T1+R1 has governed the
entire world from initial founders, instead of being switched on at a legacy birth?
This tests dependence on inherited world history/switch timing. It does NOT isolate
N from W/C, identify a rescue policy, or establish indefinite survival.

Frozen basis
R1/T1 implementation and completed evidence at
5d3a8efaf70efaefefaa19329e8d069dfe9b54ee. No new biological code. R1 on both arms;
mode0 original ordered transport versus mode24 existing synchronous T1 transport.
No feedback, resource intervention, extra mixing, rate/cost/gene/gate changes,
prepared nursery, manual birth selection, or resolution search.

One experiment, six trajectories
Three original seeds12011,4242,7301; two arms each, initialized from tick0 with each
seed's original juvenile-allocation30/evidence/matrix-SEED-1.json config. Both arms
must have identical initial full physical hashes/configs, differing only in the
explicit transport mode. Apply mode24 before the FIRST world step and inherit it
for every subsequent plant, not only founders or a chosen descendant.
Each run ends at12288h or a preregistered correctness/resource/capacity stop. This
matches the earlier ordinary observation horizon; no extension chosen from results.
Use all seeds and all births, no exclusion based on outcome. Do not pair descendants
by ID across arms: birth times, parents and occupancy can differ. Report within-seed
whole-world outcomes and complete lineage-resolved descendant records.

Readouts fixed before execution
1. For EVERY paid descendant, budget F, direct upkeep/tip respiration, repair input,
turnover, repair yield loss, construction, net mobile C, W/N uptake, leaf water,
actual photo demand, same-light fully-hydrated demand, leaf photon receipts, and
living/connected/root/leaf counts in first0–96 and0–768h, last576–768h when available.
Only births <=11520 receive a complete768h budget; later births are explicitly
right-censored, not discarded or counted as successful. Continue observing all
plants to world12288; exact death time or survival censoring is retained.
2. Log local C/N/W and simultaneous whole mobile pools for the first third/fourth
root and first leaf payment/refusal/expiry. Log actual short resources at payment
and at expiry separately from previous blocked label. Report age4,age16 N-only
root blocks and leaf N-only blocks, including absent proposals/failed occupancy.
No intervention or alternate local payment rule is introduced.
3. Whole-world lineage: every paid birth, maturity32 timing, offspring-producing
parents by generation, deaths, living generation counts and any generation3 birth;
founder reserve/deposit timings and other-plant birth tradeoffs. Finite-horizon
survival or a single later generation is not sustained replacement.
4. Report daily and cumulative carbon margins; positive cumulative budget with
negative final192h is classified as deteriorating, not steady-state viability.

Discriminating interpretation, fixed in advance
Persistence of N-only local shortfalls with sufficient whole pools, no new leaf
packets, and negative late carbon margins under fresh T1 would support a genuine
limitation of T1 plus the existing paid-growth/environment rules. A materially
different fresh-T1 pattern would show that legacy-history switched-birth results
cannot be generalized. Because the resulting lineages/environments differ, neither
outcome decomposes adult switch transients from longer historical ecology; this
experiment answers the necessary external-validity question first. Report mixed
seed outcomes as mixed; no pooled independence claim, significance threshold or
post-hoc successful-seed selection. Do not reinstate construction feedback from
this result alone. No guarantee that any cohort reaches net-positive operation.

Instrumentation and validation
Use existing observer arithmetic where sufficient; any additional observer writes
only separate diagnostics. Verify added observation does not change physical hashes
against existing golden baseline checkpoints and completed comparison checkpoints
before the long runs. Original binary-state/conservation/positivity/allocation
residual guards stay unchanged. Reuse existing validated numerical kernels; no
repeat of all kernel fixtures solely for this experiment. Save each complete day's
state and hourly hashes. One saved-state restart per arm/seed at tick6144 must
match uninterrupted continuation for the next96h; these are correctness checks,
not additional treatment samples. A mismatch stops dependent biological inference.
