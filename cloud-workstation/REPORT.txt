LINUX RESEARCH WORKSTATION 0.1.0 — LOCAL, UNPROMOTED

Result
A new evidence workspace extends the existing Godot lab instead of replacing its
controls. Six F1 runs expose paired world outcomes, common-hour resource partitions,
all lineage/death/maturity events, per-plant carbon budgets and actual binary
checkpoint replay. Reproduction commands, source paths and distinct model/check
statuses are accessible in the app. The existing live lab remains separate and
starts paused with local control OFF. Switching panes, STOP and close revoke
control; reopen remembers navigation only, never computation or access permission.

Scope choice
Full F1 comparisons and replay were prioritized. W27 and original candidate30 have
explicit model/provenance reports and reproduction instructions; their different
record schemas are not coerced into the F1 binary adapter. No simulation rules,
rates, genes, numerical kernels, reproductive gate or Windows W27 default changed.
The96h mechanism assay was NOT run. UI replays are exact checks of already archived
trajectories, not new survival experiments. No viable-lineage or promotion claim.
