Why births are later/fewer: measured budgets, then interpretation
F1 births were legacy3/5/3 versus T1 1/4/1; no generation3 in either arm. T1 is a
new simultaneous transport contract, not simply a bug fix preserving physiology.
It changes local C/N/W access, architecture and the subsequent shared environment.
All six T1 founders reached32 paid nodes by hour165, versus69–118 in controls.
This modest gate delay cannot alone explain first births8198/998/7238 versus
838/678/806. Mature savings can still be refunded for maintenance; gross reserve
deposits are not seed output, nor is total mobile C immediately spendable reserve.

Founder1 is the informative paired loss in all three worlds: T1 leaf counts are
4/5/4 versus5/6/5, light receipts983/1286/983 versus1272/1601/1296 model E,
and lifetime fixation3.394/4.348/3.396 versus4.361/5.237/4.435 model C.
Its N uptake is higher in all three T1 arms, so these reproductive losses cannot
be labeled whole-founder N starvation. T1 operating C is more negative despite
lower maintenance/repair expenditure. In seed12011,0.799 C was deposited into
reserve but0.774 refunded, with no seed release; control deposits1.060/refunds0.951
supported two0.051 C releases. This connects less realized carbon income and
maintenance competition to reduced reproductive funding. These are coupled observed
budgets, not isolated causal proof that the missing leaf alone causes the change.
Negative lifetime operation also spends initial endowment; it is not proof of a
negative current rate (several final192h margins are positive).

T1 preserves founder0 in12011/4242 and shifts first parentage to founder0 in

Censoring versus sustainability
T1's older4242 grandchildren die at ages2274 and1519. Its youngest is alive at
age1018,12 paid nodes, no offspring; its recent operating margin is positive.
That is real short-term maintenance evidence, but neither inevitable death nor
sustained replacement follows. Its siblings and the7301 child show that positive
early budgets can precede death. Three seed families, unequal offspring exposure
and a finite12288h window limit inference; descendants are not independent trials.
The late survivor is not evidence that longer running will solve maturation.

Reassessing the one allocation alternative
The earlier proposed local construction-feedback policy was NEVER implemented:
its original preflight stopped on ordered transport's representation dependence.
R1/T1 resolved the tested ordering problems separately. Under T1, the old third-
root stall clears at age5 in all11 switched-birth cohorts, yet the fourth-root
front is N-only blocked at age16. F1 also records age16 N-only blocks in all six
new descendants, five leaf N-only refusals and no newly paid leaf packets. These
observations confirm local access constraints, not that more root priority helps.

The minimally defensible alternative remains the previously specified local,
expiring signal that defers optional stem/leaf payments while a reachable root
front lacks only N. Maintenance stays first, transport/bills/deadlines stay fixed,
and no N is pumped, reserved, created or taken from other plants. Withholding a
stem payment can preserve N and avoid another compartment; it only indirectly
addresses the root bill, and may arrive too late or find nothing useful to defer.
Suppressing already-unaffordable leaves saves nothing. Suppressing affordable
shoot growth risks less future light capture, lost buds and continued tip costs;
adding roots adds construction/maintenance costs. Those risks matter more after
F1's founder carbon/light loss, not less. A whole-world application to founders
could worsen the very birth deficit it is intended to cure.

Earlier causal evidence separates constraints: target N deprivation reduced gen1
root development; removing competitors' W uptake kept all six old gen2 targets
alive2040h but none mature, at real cost to competitors. Removing only competitors'
late N uptake did not rescue roots or death; it did not release their stored N.
The switched T1 cohort study improved roots in9/11 but left five operating deficits,
all persisting in the final192h. More roots alone is therefore not a fitness result.
Neither that evidence nor local stock arithmetic establishes a free allocation
improvement. Keeping the existing model and documenting lack of replacement is
currently better supported than implementing a rescue policy.

One conditional, high-information test — proposed only, not authorized or run
If allocation mechanism remains a research priority after workstation review,
replace (do not silently resume) the old registration with ONE short matched
96h OFF/ON focal-policy assay at all six actual F1 T1 descendant births. Freeze
T1/24+R1 and the previous local signal semantics; leave every other plant unchanged.
This tests the current fourth-root bottleneck, not the already-resolved third root.
Before biology, require exact OFF replay, canonical/reverse/save-state agreement,
message delay/expiry/dead-edge checks and unchanged resource guards. Keep existing
serial ownership,8GiB headroom and600s per-invocation bounds; no horizon extension.
Read out the causal sequence: reachable signal -> withheld nonroot N payment ->
more N at the blocked front -> an additional locally paid root by96h. Record
light/fixation, operating C, unpaid upkeep and expired proposals as costs.
Stop on any correctness failure. If no cohort completes that chain, reject this
policy for this early bottleneck; do not tune or lengthen the run. If gains entail
lower operating C or more unpaid upkeep in any cohort, retain the tradeoff and
stop escalation toward a survival claim. A complete chain with no such observed
cost supports mechanism review only, never automatic longer trials or promotion.
A negative result does not exclude every possible allocation law. This assay is
higher information than another survival extension because it tests the missing
mechanistic link before accepting the added policy complexity.

User-facing version limits
W27 is the preserved reviewed baseline. Candidate30 changes savings timing only
and remains unpromoted; T1+R1 is a separate experimental numerical/model contract.
The amounts are model units, not calibrated plant measurements. Mutation is off;
N transport equalizes packet amounts rather than defined concentrations; toy
carbon mineralization is not a complete nutrient cycle, oxygen is absent, and
old/dead stores and tissue can retain N and occupy space. Current genes, finite

is not biological validation. Selected canonical windows are not universal proofs;
24/48-substep cohort agreement is not exact trajectory convergence. CPU/native
and exact physical replay evidence is strong; historical strict JSON metric
failures remain separately recorded. Hardware GPU/Windows checks remain unavailable.
